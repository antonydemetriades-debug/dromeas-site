# Contact us

We'd love to hear from you. Write to us by email and we'll reply as soon as we can, normally within a few working days.

- **Questions, feedback and help with the app:** [contact@dromeasnews.com](mailto:contact@dromeasnews.com)
- **Privacy and your data** (download, correct or delete your data): [privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com)
- **Publishers:** to ask about how your headlines appear in Dromeas, or to have your outlet removed, write to [publishers@dromeasnews.com](mailto:publishers@dromeasnews.com). We act on removal requests promptly.

## Who runs Dromeas News

Dromeas News is run by Antonios Demetriades, Prague, Czech Republic.

## Where the news comes from

Dromeas is a news aggregator. Every headline in the app is shown with the name of the outlet that published it, and links to the outlet's own page for the full article. Dromeas doesn't write or edit the news. Bias ratings come from [Media Bias Fact Check](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/).
