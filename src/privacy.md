# Privacy policy

*Version 1 · last updated {{updated}}*

This policy explains what information Dromeas News ("Dromeas") uses, why, where it goes and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language. Dromeas works without an account, and most of what it learns about you never leaves your phone.

## 1. Who we are

Dromeas is run by **Antonios Demetriades**, Holeckova 26, Smíchov, 150 00 Prague 5, Czechia. He is the "controller" of your personal data: the person responsible for deciding how it is used.

For anything about your data, write to **[privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com)**. We answer within one month, as the law requires.

## 2. Dromeas in short

- **No account needed.** You can use every feature without one. An account is optional and only keeps your newsboards and settings safe.
- **No ads today, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.** We plan to show ads later, to keep Dromeas free. Before any ad appears, we'll update this policy, tell you in the app, and ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example, for ads based on your data). We never sell your personal data.
- **Your reading history stays on your phone.** It is recorded only if you say yes to "Learn from my reading", and it is never sent to us, not even with an account.
- **One phone permission only:** internet access. No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
- **Our server is in the EU** (Frankfurt, Germany).

## 3. What stays on your phone only

These are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. We never receive them (unless you have an account: see section 5 for the settings it keeps).

- **Your reading history**, only if you said yes to "Learn from my reading": for each article you open, the outlet, its bias rating, its topics and regions, and the time. It powers the You tab, relevance sorting and outlet suggestions. It keeps the last 90 days, at most 3,000 articles. Your yes or no answer is kept too.
- **Your choices:** Front Page outlets, newsboards, blocked and subscription outlets, display and sort settings.
- **Your region,** as one of six broad areas (for example "Western Europe"), used to suggest starting points. Never your internet address or location.
- **A random app number,** made when you first open Dromeas. It isn't linked to your phone's hardware, your name or your Google account. Section 4 explains what it is used for.
- **Small notes that keep the app working smoothly.** None of them says anything about what you read:
    - which tips you've already seen or turned off, so they don't repeat;
    - the first days you opened the app, so bias notes and outlet suggestions stay quiet during your first two days;
    - the date bias notes come back after you tap "Show less";
    - whether you've opened an article yet, and when we last suggested an account (at most once a week);
    - when the app last sent the follower count, so it is sent at most once a day;
    - the last headlines loaded, so Dromeas still shows news when you're offline.
- **Pictures and logos already seen,** at most 300 for 3 days, so they show offline.

To remove them: Settings → Clear reading history, or uninstall Dromeas (or Android Settings → Apps → Dromeas → Storage → Clear storage), which deletes everything on the phone.

## 4. What reaches our server, with or without an account

A few features need our server. None of them needs your name or email.

- **Words you type to create a newsboard** ("＋ Create new" or "Search with AI"). They go to our server and to Anthropic's AI (section 6) to work out the topic. To keep to the limit of 50 AI checks per phone a day, we note the time and your random app number; that note is deleted after 2 days. If your words start a new shared topic, they are kept with that topic, without your app number, and can appear in our monthly topics report. Please don't type personal details.
- **Ready-made topics and starting points** are answered instantly: nothing you typed goes to the AI and nothing is noted against your app number.
- **Words you type in AI outlet search** go to our server and to Anthropic in the same way. The note of the time and your app number is deleted after 2 days.
- **Outlet nominations:** the outlet's name and website, your optional reason, and your app number, so the app can show you the outcome. Your request is deleted 90 days after we decide; the outlet's name and website stay on our list of nominations.
- **The follower count:** once a day the app tells our server which newsboard topics your phone follows, with your app number. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of the number, once per topic per month, and deletes it after 2 months. It tells us how many people follow each topic. No name or email is attached, and nobody can read it through the app.
- **Your region, on first open:** our server uses your internet address to look up your country in a list kept on our own server (DB-IP), then returns only the broad area. The address isn't stored for this.
- **News itself:** the app downloads headlines and stories from our server. These requests carry no account details. Like any website, our hosting keeps short technical logs that include internet addresses, for security and fault-finding. These logs are kept for 1 day on our current hosting plan (7 days on a paid plan).
- **"Find out about bias on this story"** uses fixed questions and the outlets' headlines. Nothing about you is sent.

## 5. If you create an account

An account is optional. It keeps your newsboards and settings, so they come back after a reinstall or on another phone.

- **Signing in by email:** your email address. We email you a 6-digit code through our email service, Resend (section 6).
- **Continue with Google:** Google tells us your email address, your name and your profile picture. Google also knows you signed in to Dromeas (Google's own privacy policy applies).
- **Sign-in records:** our sign-in service keeps when you signed in and from which internet address, to keep your account secure.
- **What the account keeps:** your newsboards and their settings, Front Page outlets, blocked and subscription outlets, display, appearance and sort choices, your answers to outlet suggestions, tips seen, your region area, your nominations list and random app number (so nominations follow you), and when each newsboard was last used (for the 30-day check). **Never your reading history.**

**Delete my account** (Settings → Account) deletes the account and everything it keeps, straight away. Dromeas keeps working on your phone without an account. Two items aren't tied to the account, only to your random app number, so they end on their own schedule: nomination requests (90 days after our decision) and the follower count (2 months). See also [Deleting your account](/delete-account/).

## 6. Outside services we use

These companies help run Dromeas. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.

| Service | What it does for Dromeas | What it receives about you | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Our server, database and sign-in | Everything in sections 4 and 5 | EU (Frankfurt); Supabase is a US company |
| Anthropic | The AI that understands newsboard and outlet-search words, and tags and groups headlines | The words you type for those two features; never your app number, name or email | United States |
| Resend | Sends the sign-in code email, and our replies to your emails | Your email address and the email itself | United States |
| Google | Continue with Google (only if you use it) | Your Google sign-in | EU and United States |
| ImprovMX | Passes on emails you send to our addresses (privacy@, publishers@) to the inbox where we read them | Your email address and your message | United States |
| Google (Gmail) | The inbox where we read and answer your emails | Your email address, your message and our reply | EU and United States |
| GitHub | Our back office: reviewing nominations and the monthly topics report | Outlet names, websites and reasons from nominations; topic words and follower totals (no app numbers) | United States |

**News outlets:** article pictures load straight from each outlet's servers, and "View full article" opens the outlet's own page in a browser tab inside Dromeas. The outlet then sees your internet address, and its own cookies and privacy policy apply.

**Media Bias Fact Check and DB-IP:** we download their lists (bias ratings, and countries by internet address) to our server. Nothing about you is sent to them.

**Data outside the EU:** where data goes to the United States, it is protected by the safeguards EU law allows, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 7. Why we may use your data

EU law (the GDPR) lets us use personal data only for a stated reason, called a legal basis.

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Your account: sign-in codes, keeping your newsboards and settings | Providing the service you asked for (contract) |
| Understanding the words you type for newsboards and outlet search | Providing the service you asked for (contract) |
| Daily limits per app number; sign-in and server security records | Our legitimate interest in preventing misuse and controlling AI costs |
| Outlet nominations and telling you the outcome | Our legitimate interest in improving the outlet list |
| The follower count | Our legitimate interest in deciding which topics to offer |
| Your region on first open | Our legitimate interest in suggesting relevant starting points |
| Your reading history (on your phone only) | Your consent, which you can withdraw any time in Settings |

Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object (section 9).

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or give it to you in a file you can take elsewhere. You can object to any use based on our legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

- **Download my data** (Settings → Account): one file with everything your account keeps and everything kept on this phone, including your reading history and your random app number.
- **Delete my account** (Settings → Account): deletes your account at once.
- **Clear reading history** and the **Learn from my reading** switch (Settings).

For anything else, write to [privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com). Without an account we can't tell who you are, so please include your random app number (it's in the Download my data file); we use it only to find the records linked to it.

**Complaints:** you can complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, [uoou.gov.cz](https://uoou.gov.cz), or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

## 10. Minimum age

Dromeas is for people aged **16 or older**. Creating an account confirms that you are. Dromeas doesn't ask for your age or check it, so we only learn that a user is under 16 if someone tells us. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, write to [privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com) with the account's email address; we'll delete the account and everything it keeps. For data linked only to a random app number, include that number (it's in the Download my data file). Uninstalling Dromeas deletes everything kept on the phone.

## 11. Security and changes to this policy

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted. Our database is locked so the app's public key can't read other readers' data: each account can read only its own saved settings.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
