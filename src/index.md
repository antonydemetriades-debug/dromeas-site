<p class="lede">Pick your sources, track topics with custom newsboards, and spot political bias at a glance.</p>

- **Front Page:** the latest headlines from the outlets you choose, across the political and geographic spectrum.
- **Newsboards:** describe any story or topic in your own words, and Dromeas keeps a feed of it for you, from every outlet or only the ones you pick.
- **Full coverage:** see how many outlets cover a story, compare left, center and right, and spot one-sided coverage.

Dromeas never copies articles: it shows headlines and links to the outlet's own page. Bias ratings come from Media Bias Fact Check.
