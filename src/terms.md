## 2. What Dromeas is and isn't

Dromeas is a **news aggregator**. It collects headlines, short snippets and pictures that news outlets publish in their public feeds, groups articles about the same event, and shows each outlet's political lean so you can see how one-sided coverage is.

Dromeas **is not a news publisher**. We don't write, edit or check the news, and we don't rewrite headlines: every headline is the outlet's own. Showing an outlet doesn't mean we agree with it or vouch for it, and leaving one out doesn't mean we think less of it.

Dromeas is **not professional advice**. Nothing in it is legal, financial, medical or other advice. For decisions that matter, check the original source and ask a qualified person.

## 3. News, bias ratings and AI come from others and can be wrong

- **The news** comes from the outlets. We can't guarantee it is accurate, complete or up to date, and an outlet may change or remove an article after we show its headline.
- **Bias ratings** come from Media Bias Fact Check, an independent organisation. They rate whole outlets, not single articles, and they are opinions. Outlets it doesn't rate show "Not rated".
- **AI** helps run Dromeas: it sorts headlines into topics and regions, groups articles about the same event, understands the words you type for a newsboard or an outlet search, picks the stories for each newsboard, and answers the questions under "Find out about bias on this story". AI can make mistakes. It may put a story in the wrong place, group two different events, miss a story, or give an answer that is incomplete or wrong. AI answers are not checked by a person before you see them.
- **Coverage counts and balance notes** count only the outlets in Dromeas, which is a small selection. They describe our list, not all the news in the world.

If something looks wrong, please tell us at [publishers@dromeasnews.com](mailto:publishers@dromeasnews.com) (for news and outlets) or [privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com) (for your data).

## 4. Articles belong to their publishers

Headlines, snippets, pictures and articles belong to the outlets that published them (or to whoever they licensed them from). Dromeas shows only the headline, a short snippet and a picture, always with the outlet's name, and links to the outlet's own site for the full article. We never copy full articles into the app.

You may read and share links to articles for your own personal use. Don't copy, republish or sell the outlets' content, or Dromeas's lists of stories and ratings, without the owner's permission.

**Outlets:** if you publish news and don't want to appear in Dromeas, or want something changed, write to [publishers@dromeasnews.com](mailto:publishers@dromeasnews.com). We normally act on removal requests within one working day.

The Dromeas name, logo and app design are ours. The bias ratings are licensed from Media Bias Fact Check and may not be copied out of the app.

## 5. Who can use Dromeas, and your account

Dromeas is for people aged **16 or older**. Creating an account confirms that you are.

You don't need an account. If you create one (by email code or Continue with Google), please:

- use an email address that is yours;
- keep access to that email or Google account safe: anyone who can read it can sign in to your Dromeas account;
- have only one account per person, and don't share it or sell it.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete my account, or as described at [Deleting your account](/delete-account/). Dromeas keeps working on your phone without one.

## 6. Using Dromeas fairly

**Words you type.** When you create a newsboard or search for outlets, the words you type go to our AI to work out what you mean. If they start a new shared topic, they are kept with that topic and can be seen by us in our reports (never with your name). So please:

- don't type personal details about yourself or anyone else;
- don't type anything unlawful, hateful, threatening or sexually explicit.

**Nominations.** When you nominate an outlet, you agree that we may use the name, website and reason you give to decide whether to add it. We review every nomination and may say no, with a reason.

**Daily limits.** To keep AI costs under control, some features have daily limits per phone (for example 50 newsboard previews and 50 AI outlet searches a day). The app tells you when you reach one and when you can try again.

**Please don't:**

- try to get around the daily limits, for example by resetting the app again and again or by automated requests;
- collect Dromeas's content with bots or scripts, copy our lists of stories, outlets or ratings, or build another service from them;
- interfere with the app or our server, look for ways into other readers' data, or overload the service;
- use Dromeas for anything unlawful.

**If someone misuses Dromeas,** we may block their app number or suspend or close their account, and delete what they typed. Where we can and it's fair to, we'll tell them why first. If you think we got it wrong, write to [privacy@dromeasnews.com](mailto:privacy@dromeasnews.com).
