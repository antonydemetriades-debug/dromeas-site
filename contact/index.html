Who runs Dromeas News

Where the news comes from

Dromeas is a news aggregator. Every headline in the app is shown with the name of the outlet that published it, and links to the outlet’s own page for the full article. Dromeas doesn’t write or edit the news. Bias ratings come from Media Bias Fact Check.
