Delete your Dromeas account

Dromeas News, by Antonios Demetriades

An account is optional in Dromeas. If you made one, you can delete it at any time, in two ways.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dromeas and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. Dromeas keeps working on your phone without an account.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to privacy@dromeasnews.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my Dromeas account”. We’ll delete it and confirm by reply, within one month at the latest (usually within a few days).

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, and your name and picture if you used Continue with Google.
  • Everything the account keeps: your newsboards and their settings, Front Page outlets, blocked and subscription outlets, display choices, and your list of nominations.
  • Your sign-in records.

What is not deleted at once

  • Outlet nominations you sent stay linked to your phone’s random app number (not to your account) and are deleted 90 days after we decide on them. The outlet’s name and website stay on our list of nominations.
  • The follower count (a scrambled form of your app number, per topic and month) is deleted after 2 months.
  • What’s on your phone (including your reading history, which is never sent to us) stays until you uninstall Dromeas or clear its storage.

To have nominations and the follower count removed sooner, include your random app number in your email (it’s in the file from Settings → Account → Download my data).
